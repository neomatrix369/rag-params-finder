# What did the best settings change?

Ask the same question under two different settings and the search returns different chunks with different similarity scores.

The demo compares the best-scoring and the worst-scoring configuration on one Fed Loan (Pell Grant) question: the top chunk each one returns, and the **similarity-score** gap between them. Scores are normalised across the runs shown, so read them as a ranking, not as absolute numbers.

A higher similarity score means the chunk is closer to the question in embedding space. It does not prove the chunk is the right answer; judging that is up to you. Relevance metrics are planned.

The side-by-side view is step 3 of the guided Walkthru.
